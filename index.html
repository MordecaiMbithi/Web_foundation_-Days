About QuickNotes

QuickNotes is a lightweight browser notebook designed to capture everyday tasks, thoughts, and quick reminders seamlessly. It provides a clean, focused workspace so you never lose track of important ideas throughout your study sessions.

How to use QuickNotes

  1. Type your thought or reminder into the "New note" field.
  2. Click the "Add Note" button or press Enter to record it.
  3. Review your saved items directly in the "Your notes" list below.

Features

  • Fast, distraction-free input interface
  • Instant local list updates
  • Accessible semantic HTML structure
